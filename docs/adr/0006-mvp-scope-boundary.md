# The MVP is a supplier-cost and margin watchdog, not inventory, purchasing or bookkeeping

The MVP covers one loop: supplier invoice in, checked and reviewed, prices recorded, price alerts and below-target menu items out. It deliberately does not do inventory or stock counts, purchasing and ordering, POS integration or sales mix, accounting export, or act as the venue's legal bookkeeping archive; it keeps invoice originals for review and disputes only. A reasonable reader would expect a restaurant cost tool to do at least stock and POS; we leave them out because each needs integrations or daily staff routines that small venues do not keep, and the invoice-to-margin loop is valuable on its own. Accounting export (Fortnox, Visma) is the first candidate after the MVP.
